Gangster movie set in England in the 1980s. It was alright; there was nothing that really annoyed me. The heavy use of music in the first 10 minutes was interesting—in a film nowadays, this probably would have been much more suppressed. But besides that, the reason why I wouldn't rank it any higher, there was nothing that really excited me about the movie. **6/10.**
