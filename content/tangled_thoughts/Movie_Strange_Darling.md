I liked the opening. Movie has some really cool shots. In general, it felt very cinematic, with great cinematography. I also liked the way it was split into chapters; it felt very Tarantino-esque. The music during the opening chase scene annoyed me, though, and the dialogue was rough. Tarantino wouldn't have written dialogue this bad. If you ignore the dialogue, it's a *9/10*; with the dialogue included, I'd give it a **6/10**.
