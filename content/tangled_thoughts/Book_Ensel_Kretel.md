I liked it even more than his [previous work](/tangled_thoughts/book_13_leben_kaeptn_blaubar/), but this book is probably not for everyone, particularly because of the author's frequent interjections, where he tends to rant about various topics. I absolutely love those rants, they feel like love letters to creativity, completely unbound by the story or any particular goal. Instead, they wander through all kinds of subjects. I love them, but be warned: this book is much less story-focused.
